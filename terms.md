# Art History Terms

## Iconographic Types

### Christus triumphans
A cross depicting a living Christ, showing him triumphant over death.

### Christus patiens
A cross depicting a dead Christ, emphasizing his suffering and sacrifice.

### Tondo
A circular painting or relief sculpture. The term comes from the Italian word "rotondo" (round).

## Names

### Francesco
方濟各 - Francis (as in St. Francis of Assisi).

## Materials

### Pietra serena
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pietra_serena)

Blue-gray sandstone used extensively in Renaissance Florence for architectural elements.

## Painting Styles

### Pompeian Styles
Four periods distinguished in ancient Roman mural painting, originally delineated by German archaeologist August Mau from excavations at Pompeii:
- **First Style** (Incrustation, c. 200-80 BC): Imitation of marble veneer
- **Second Style** (Architectural, c. 80-20 BC): Illusionistic architecture and depth
- **Third Style** (Ornamental, c. 20 BC-50 AD): Delicate, decorative motifs
- **Fourth Style** (Intricate, c. 50-79 AD): Combines elements of previous styles

## Religious and Ritual

### Dionysian Mysteries
A ritual of ancient Greece and Rome, sometimes using trance-inducing techniques. The cult of Dionysus (Bacchus) provided liberation for marginalized groups. The mysteries are depicted in the famous frescoes of the [Villa of the Mysteries](artworks/VillaOfTheMysteriesFrescoes.md).

## Latin Phrases

### Cave Canem
Latin for "Beware of the Dog," a warning phrase found on mosaic floors in Roman houses, most famously at the [House of the Tragic Poet](artworks/HouseOfTheTragicPoet.md) in Pompeii.

## Historical Events

### Battle of Issus
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Issus)

A battle in southern Anatolia on 5 November 333 BC between the Hellenic League led by Alexander the Great and the Achaemenid Empire led by Darius III. Alexander's victory forced Darius to flee, leaving behind his family and treasury. This battle is the subject of the famous [Alexander Mosaic](artworks/AlexanderMosaic.md) found in the House of the Faun.

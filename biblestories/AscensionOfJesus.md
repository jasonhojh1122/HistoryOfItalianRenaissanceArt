# Ascension of Jesus

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascension_of_Jesus)

**Source**: self-research

## Summary

The Christian belief that Jesus ascended bodily to Heaven forty days after his Resurrection. Depicted in the central dome of Saint Mark's Basilica, showing Christ in glory rising to heaven while the Apostles watch from below.

## Biblical Reference

**Book(s)**: Luke, Acts
**Chapters/Verses**: Luke 24:50-53, Acts 1:9-11

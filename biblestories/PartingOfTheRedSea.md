# Parting of the Red Sea

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parting_of_the_Red_Sea)

**Source**: self-research

## Summary

An episode in the Exodus story where Moses, leading the Israelites out of Egypt, parts the Red Sea to allow their passage. When the pursuing Egyptian army follows, the waters return and drown them. This miraculous event demonstrates God's power and protection of the Israelites during their escape from slavery.

## Biblical Reference

**Book(s)**: Exodus
**Chapters/Verses**: Exodus 14

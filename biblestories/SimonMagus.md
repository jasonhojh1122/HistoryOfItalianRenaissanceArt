## Summary

Simon Magus was a religious figure whose confrontation with the Apostle Peter is recorded in Acts. He tried to purchase the power of the Holy Spirit from the Apostles, giving rise to the term "simony." Later apocryphal tradition recounts his attempt to fly before Emperor Nero in Rome, only to be struck down by the prayers of Saints Peter and Paul.

## Biblical Reference

**Book(s)**: Acts of the Apostles; apocryphal Acts of Peter
**Chapters/Verses**: Acts 8:9-24

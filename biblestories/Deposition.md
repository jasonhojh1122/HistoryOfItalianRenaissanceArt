# Deposition (Descent from the Cross)

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descent_from_the_Cross)

**Source**: self-research

## Summary

The scene of Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus taking Christ down from the cross after his crucifixion. It is the 13th Station of the Cross and one of the Seven Sorrows of the Virgin Mary.

## Biblical Reference

**Book(s)**: All four Gospels
**Chapters/Verses**: Matthew 27:57-60, Mark 15:42-46, Luke 23:50-54, John 19:38-42

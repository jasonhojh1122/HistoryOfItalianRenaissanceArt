# Presentation of Mary

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presentation_of_Mary)

**Source**: self-research

## Summary

According to tradition, Mary was presented at the Temple in Jerusalem at age three, where she was dedicated to God and lived until her betrothal. A popular subject in Renaissance art.

## Biblical Reference

**Book(s)**: Protoevangelium of James (apocryphal)

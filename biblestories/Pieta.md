## Summary

The Pietà is a subject in Christian art depicting the Virgin Mary cradling the mortal body of Jesus Christ after his Descent from the Cross. It is a specific form of the Lamentation of Christ in which Jesus is mourned by the Virgin Mary alone. The term comes from Italian meaning "pity" or "compassion."

## Biblical Reference

**Book(s)**: Gospels
**Chapters/Verses**: Matthew 27:57-61, Mark 15:42-47, Luke 23:50-56, John 19:38-42

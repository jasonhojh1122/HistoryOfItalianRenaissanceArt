# Giuseppe Porta (Salviati)

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Porta)

**Born**: 1520, Castelnuovo di Garfagnana
**Died**: 1575, Venice
**Source**: self-research

Italian painter of the late-Renaissance period, active mostly in Venice. Contributed three ceiling tondi to the Biblioteca Marciana: "Virtue Despises Fortune," "Art, Mercury and Pluto," and "Pallas and Hercules."

## Artworks

Works at [Biblioteca Marciana](../locations/BibliotecaMarciana.md): ceiling tondi.

# Baldassare Longhena

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldassare_Longhena)

**Born**: 1598, Venice
**Died**: 1682, Venice
**Source**: self-research

Italian architect, one of the greatest exponents of Baroque architecture in Venice. His style is characterized by monumentality, skillful use of light and shadow, and an understanding of Venetian aesthetics. The Salute is his masterpiece.

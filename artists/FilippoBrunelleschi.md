# Filippo Brunelleschi

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filippo_Brunelleschi)

**Born**: 1377, Florence, Republic of Florence
**Died**: April 15, 1446, Florence

Italian architect, designer, goldsmith, and sculptor. Considered a founding father of Renaissance architecture and the first modern engineer. He developed linear perspective and in 1421 became the first person to receive a patent in the Western world.

## Artworks

### [Brunelleschi Crucifix](../artworks/BrunelleschiCrucifix.md)
- **Location**: [Santa Maria Novella](../locations/SantaMariaNovella.md), Florence (Gondi Chapel)
- **Medium**: Polychrome painted pearwood
- The only surviving wooden sculpture by Brunelleschi, described as "probably the first Renaissance work in the history of art"

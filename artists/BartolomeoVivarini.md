# Bartolomeo Vivarini

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolomeo_Vivarini)

**Born**: c. 1432, Murano
**Died**: c. 1499, Venice
**Source**: self-research

Italian Renaissance painter known to have worked from 1450 to 1499. Part of the prominent Vivarini family of painters from Murano.

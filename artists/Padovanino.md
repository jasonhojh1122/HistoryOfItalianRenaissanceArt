# Padovanino

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padovanino)

**Born**: 1588, Padua
**Died**: 1649, Venice
**Source**: self-research

Alessandro Leone Varotari, known as Il Padovanino, was a Venetian painter of the late-Mannerist and early-Baroque Venetian school. Repainted Zelotti's "Astrology" tondo in the Biblioteca Marciana ceiling.

Works at [Biblioteca Marciana](../locations/BibliotecaMarciana.md): "Astrology" ceiling tondo (remake).

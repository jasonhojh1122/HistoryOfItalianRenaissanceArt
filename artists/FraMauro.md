# Fra Mauro

- **Born**: Unknown, Venice
- **Died**: 1459, Venice
- **Source**: self-research

Venetian cartographer and monk who created the Fra Mauro map around 1450, considered the greatest memorial of medieval cartography. The map is housed in the Biblioteca Marciana.

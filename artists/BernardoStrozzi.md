# Bernardo Strozzi

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernardo_Strozzi)

**Born**: 1581, Genoa
**Died**: 1644, Venice
**Source**: self-research

Italian Baroque painter and engraver. Named il Cappuccino and il Prete Genovese. Repainted Giulio Licinio's "Sculpture" tondo in the Biblioteca Marciana ceiling, working in Venice in the latter part of his career.

## Artworks

Works at [Biblioteca Marciana](../locations/BibliotecaMarciana.md): "Sculpture" ceiling tondo (remake).

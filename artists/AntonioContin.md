# Antonio Contin

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_of_Sighs)

**Died**: c. 1600
**Source**: self-research

Italian architect who designed the Bridge of Sighs. His uncle Antonio da Ponte designed the famous Rialto Bridge. Both bridges are iconic landmarks of Venice.

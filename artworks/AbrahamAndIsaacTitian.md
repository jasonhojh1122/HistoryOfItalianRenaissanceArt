# Abraham and Isaac

- **Artist**: [Titian](../artists/Titian.md)
- **Location**: [Santa Maria della Salute](../locations/SantaMariaDellaSalute.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas (ceiling)
- **Date**: 1542-1544
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Binding of Isaac](../biblestories/BindingOfIsaac.md) - God tests Abraham by commanding him to sacrifice his son Isaac. An angel stops Abraham at the last moment.

## Description

Ceiling painting in the sacristy, part of the trio with David and Goliath and Cain and Abel. Depicts the moment of Abraham's sacrifice being stayed by an angel. Demonstrates Titian's mastery of dramatic perspective.

# Madonna and Child

- **Artist**: [Bartolomeo Vivarini](../artists/BartolomeoVivarini.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Tempera on panel
- **Date**: Mid-15th century
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Madonna and Child](../biblestories/MadonnaAndChild.md) - The Virgin Mary with the infant Jesus.

## Description

One of two Madonna and Child paintings by Bartolomeo Vivarini in the collection, representing the transition from Late Gothic to Early Renaissance in Venice.

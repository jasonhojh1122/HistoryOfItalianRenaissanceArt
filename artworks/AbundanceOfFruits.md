# Abundance of Fruits

- **Artist**: Agostino Corsini
- **Location**: [Trevi Fountain](../locations/TreviFountain.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Marble sculpture
- **Date**: c. 1735-1762
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

One of four allegorical statues adorning the attic level of the fountain, representing the abundance of fruits. The figure holds a horn of plenty (cornucopia), symbolizing the bounty that water brings to agriculture.

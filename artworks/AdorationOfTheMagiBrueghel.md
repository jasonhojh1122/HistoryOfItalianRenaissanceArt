# The Adoration of the Magi

- **Artist**: [Pieter Brueghel the Younger](../artists/PieterBrueghelTheYounger.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: Late 16th century
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Adoration of the Magi](../biblestories/AdorationOfTheMagi.md) - The visit of the Magi (wise men) to the infant Jesus, bringing gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

## Description

A variation of his father Pieter Bruegel the Elder's work with miniaturist detail. Part of the museum's collection of Flemish works.

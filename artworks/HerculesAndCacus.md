# Hercules and Cacus

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hercules_and_Cacus)

- **Artist**: [Baccio Bandinelli](../artists/BaccioBandinelli.md)
- **Location**: [Palazzo Vecchio](../locations/PalazzoVecchio.md), Florence
- **Medium**: Marble
- **Date**: 1525-1534
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

A monumental marble sculpture standing to the right of the entrance of the Palazzo Vecchio in the Piazza della Signoria. It depicts the mythological hero Hercules subduing the fire-breathing giant Cacus. Commissioned as a pendant to Michelangelo's David.

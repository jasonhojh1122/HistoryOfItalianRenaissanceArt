# Altars of St Paul, St James, and St Clement

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Rizzo_(architect))

- **Artist**: [Antonio Rizzo](../artists/AntonioRizzo.md)
- **Location**: [Saint Mark's Basilica](../locations/SaintMarksBasilica.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Marble sculpture
- **Date**: Second half of 15th century
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

Three Renaissance altars featuring statues of the saints surrounded by elegant Renaissance tabernacles. Created by Antonio Rizzo of Verona, these works represent the transition from Gothic to Renaissance sculptural style within the basilica.

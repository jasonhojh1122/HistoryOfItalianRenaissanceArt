# St. Anthony of Padua

- **Artist**: [Alvise Vivarini](../artists/AlviseVivarini.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: Late 15th century
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

A refined, elegant work still in its original frame. Alvise was the leading Venetian artist before Giovanni Bellini.

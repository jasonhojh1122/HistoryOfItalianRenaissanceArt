# Pietà

- **Artist**: [Antonello da Messina](../artists/AntonelloDaMessina.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: c. 1474-1476
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Pietà](../biblestories/Pieta.md) - The Virgin Mary cradling the dead body of Christ after the Crucifixion.

## Description

The only work by Antonello da Messina remaining in Venice from his 1474-76 stay in the city. Originally housed in the Doge's Palace. This work was influential in introducing Flemish oil painting techniques to Venice.

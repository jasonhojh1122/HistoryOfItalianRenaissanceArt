# Crucifixion

- **Artist**: [Giovanni Bellini](../artists/GiovanniBellini.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Tempera on panel
- **Date**: 1453-1455
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Crucifixion](../biblestories/Crucifixion.md) - The death of Jesus Christ on the cross at Golgotha.

## Description

An early career work showing the influence of Andrea Mantegna, Giovanni's brother-in-law. This Crucifixion demonstrates Bellini's development before he achieved his mature style.

# The Flood

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistine_Chapel_ceiling)

- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Location**: [Sistine Chapel](../locations/SistineChapel.md), Vatican City
- **Medium**: Fresco
- **Date**: 1508-1509

## Biblical Context

[Story of Noah](../biblestories/StoryOfNoah.md) - The great flood sent by God to cleanse the earth of wickedness (Genesis 6-9).

## Description

Completed in 29 days. The scene shows the devastating flood waters rising as people desperately seek safety. The father carries the body of his drowned son—a powerful image of loss and divine judgment. One of the central narrative panels on the Sistine Chapel ceiling.

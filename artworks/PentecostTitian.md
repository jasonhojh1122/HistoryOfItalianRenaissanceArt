# The Pentecost

- **Artist**: [Titian](../artists/Titian.md)
- **Location**: [Santa Maria della Salute](../locations/SantaMariaDellaSalute.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Date**: c. 1545
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Pentecost](../biblestories/Pentecost.md) - The descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles fifty days after Easter.

## Description

A large painting in the nave depicting the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles. One of several Titian works transferred to the Salute from suppressed churches.

# Adoration of the Shepherds

- **Artist**: [Francesco Cozza](../artists/FrancescoCozza.md)
- **Location**: [Pantheon](../locations/Pantheon.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Oil painting
- **Date**: 1661
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Adoration of the Shepherds](../biblestories/AdorationOfTheShepherds.md) - The shepherds near Bethlehem visit the newborn Jesus after being summoned by an angel (Luke 2:8-20).

## Description

One of two paintings by Francesco Cozza in the Chapel of St. Joseph, depicting the shepherds coming to worship the newborn Christ child in Bethlehem.

# Amphitheatre of Pompeii

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphitheatre_of_Pompeii)

- **Artist**: Roman architects
- **Location**: [Pompeii Archaeological Park](../locations/PompeiiArchaeologicalPark.md), Campania
- **Medium**: Stone architecture
- **Date**: c. 80 BC
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

One of the oldest surviving Roman amphitheatres, built during the early Roman colony of Pompeii. It could seat approximately 20,000 spectators for gladiatorial games and other spectacles. A remarkable example of Roman engineering and public entertainment architecture.

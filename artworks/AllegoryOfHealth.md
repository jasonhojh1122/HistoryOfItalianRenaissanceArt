# Allegory of Health (Salubrity)

- **Artist**: [Filippo della Valle](../artists/FilippoDellaValle.md)
- **Location**: [Trevi Fountain](../locations/TreviFountain.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Marble sculpture
- **Date**: c. 1751-1762
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

Positioned in the right niche flanking Oceanus, this female figure holds a cup from which a serpent drinks, the traditional symbol of health. She represents the healing and life-giving properties of fresh water.

# Temple of Saturn

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Saturn)

- **Location**: [Roman Forum](../locations/RomanForum.md), Rome
- **Type**: Temple
- **Date**: Originally dedicated 497 BC; current ruins from 42 BC reconstruction
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

An ancient Roman temple to the god Saturn, standing at the foot of the Capitoline Hill at the western end of the Roman Forum. Eight impressive columns and part of the entablature remain standing. The temple housed the state treasury ([aerarium](../terms.md#aerarium)) and was the focus of the [Saturnalia](../terms.md#saturnalia) festival, one of the most popular Roman holidays.

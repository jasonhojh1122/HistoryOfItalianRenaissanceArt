# Ciborium Columns

- **Artist**: Unknown (Byzantine or Venetian craftsmen)
- **Location**: [Saint Mark's Basilica](../locations/SaintMarksBasilica.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oriental alabaster with bas-relief carvings
- **Date**: 13th century
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Madonna and Child](../biblestories/MadonnaAndChild.md) - The columns depict episodes from the lives of Christ and the Virgin Mary.

## Description

Four ornately carved alabaster columns supporting the ciborium (altar canopy) over the high altar. The columns contain 108 bas-relief scenes depicting episodes from the lives of Christ and the Virgin Mary, carved in a style that blends Byzantine and Western influences. Among the most intricate relief carvings in the basilica.

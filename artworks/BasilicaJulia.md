# Basilica Julia

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_Julia)

- **Location**: [Roman Forum](../locations/RomanForum.md), Rome
- **Type**: Civil Basilica
- **Date**: Begun 46 BC by Julius Caesar; completed by Augustus 12 AD
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

A large civil [basilica](../terms.md#basilica) that served as the headquarters for the centumviri, a judicial court dealing with matters of inheritance. The three-story marble structure was one of the largest buildings in the Forum. Today only the foundations, steps, and a few column bases and brick piers remain. Game boards scratched into the marble steps by bored Romans can still be seen.

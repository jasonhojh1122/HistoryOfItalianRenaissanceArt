# Adoration of the Magi

- **Artist**: [Francesco Cozza](../artists/FrancescoCozza.md)
- **Location**: [Pantheon](../locations/Pantheon.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Oil painting
- **Date**: 1661
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Adoration of the Magi](../biblestories/AdorationOfTheMagi.md) - The three wise men from the East follow a star to Bethlehem to present gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh to the infant Jesus (Matthew 2:1-12).

## Description

Companion piece to the Adoration of the Shepherds in the Chapel of St. Joseph, depicting the three Magi presenting gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh to the infant Jesus.

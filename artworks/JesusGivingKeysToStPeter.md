# Jesus giving the Keys to St. Peter

- **Artist**: [Lorenzo Veneziano](../artists/LorenzoVeneziano.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Tempera on panel
- **Date**: 1370
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Delivery of the Keys](../biblestories/DeliveryOfTheKeys.md) - Jesus giving Peter the keys to the kingdom of heaven.

## Description

A monumental composition with marble throne. Lorenzo Veneziano was the first Venetian painter to move away from Byzantine models toward the Gothic style.

# Pietà

- **Artist**: [Giovanni Bellini](../artists/GiovanniBellini.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: c. 1460s
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Pietà](../biblestories/Pieta.md) - The Virgin Mary cradling the dead body of Christ after the Crucifixion.

## Description

An atmospheric composition echoing Donatello's bronze relief. Part of the museum's significant collection of Bellini family works.

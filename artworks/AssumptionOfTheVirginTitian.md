# Assumption of the Virgin

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assumption_of_the_Virgin_(Titian))

- **Artist**: [Titian](../artists/Titian.md)
- **Location**: [Basilica di Santa Maria Gloriosa dei Frari](../locations/BasilicaDeiFrari.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: 1515-1518
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Assumption of Mary](../biblestories/AssumptionOfMary.md) - The belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was assumed bodily into Heaven at the end of her earthly life.

## Description

Titian's most famous painting, commissioned for the Basilica and remaining in its original position on the high altar. The largest altarpiece in Venice, with figures well over life-size. The work established Titian as the most prominent painter of 16th century Venice. Also known as the Assunta.

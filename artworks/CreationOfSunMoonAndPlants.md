# Creation of Sun, Moon and Plants

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistine_Chapel_ceiling)

- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Location**: [Sistine Chapel](../locations/SistineChapel.md), Vatican City
- **Medium**: Fresco
- **Date**: 1511

## Biblical Context

[Creation](../biblestories/CreationOfAdam.md) - God creates the heavens and the earth (Genesis 1:14-19).

## Description

Completed in 7 days. Shows God creating the sun and moon with outstretched arms, commanding the celestial bodies into existence. In the same panel, God is shown a second time from behind, creating the plants. This dual depiction of God in one scene is a compositional innovation.

- **Source**: self-research

## Mythological Context

[Aeneas](../mythology/Aeneas.md) - Trojan hero who carried his aged father Anchises on his shoulders while leading his young son Ascanius and other survivors from burning Troy to Italy.

## Description

A sculpture depicting a scene from Virgil's Aeneid, where the hero Aeneas leads his family from burning Troy, carrying his aged father Anchises on his shoulders while his young son Ascanius follows.

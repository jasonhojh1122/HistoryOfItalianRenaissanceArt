# Cain and Abel

- **Artist**: [Titian](../artists/Titian.md)
- **Location**: [Santa Maria della Salute](../locations/SantaMariaDellaSalute.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas (ceiling)
- **Date**: 1542-1544
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Cain and Abel](../biblestories/CainAndAbel.md) - The story of the first murder, in which Cain kills his brother Abel out of jealousy.

## Description

Ceiling painting showing Cain killing his brother Abel. Part of Titian's Old Testament ceiling series, renowned for its powerful foreshortening and dynamic composition.

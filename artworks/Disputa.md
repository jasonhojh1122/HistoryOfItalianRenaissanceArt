# Disputa

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disputation_of_the_Holy_Sacrament)

- **Artist**: [Raphael](../artists/Raphael.md)
- **Location**: [Apostolic Palace](../locations/ApostolicPalace.md), Vatican
- **Medium**: Fresco
- **Date**: 1509-1510

## Biblical Context

[Holy Trinity](../biblestories/HolyTrinity.md) - The theological doctrine of one God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

## Description

Also known as the "Disputation of the Holy Sacrament," this fresco depicts the Church's triumph over heresy. The composition is organized in two zones: the heavenly realm above with Christ, Mary, John the Baptist, and the saints, and the earthly realm below with theologians debating the Eucharist.

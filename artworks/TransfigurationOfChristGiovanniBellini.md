# Transfiguration of Christ

- **Artist**: [Giovanni Bellini](../artists/GiovanniBellini.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: c. 1480
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Transfiguration](../biblestories/Transfiguration.md) - Jesus revealed in glory upon a mountain, accompanied by Moses and Elijah.

## Description

Likely originally from San Salvatore or San Giobbe church. One of several works in the museum representing Giovanni Bellini's mature period.

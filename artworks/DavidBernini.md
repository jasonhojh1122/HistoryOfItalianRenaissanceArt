# David

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_(Bernini))

- **Artist**: [Gian Lorenzo Bernini](../artists/GianLorenzoBernini.md)
- **Location**: [Galleria Borghese](../locations/GalleriaBorghese.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Marble sculpture
- **Date**: 1623–1624
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[David and Goliath](../biblestories/DavidAndGoliath.md) - Young David defeats the giant Philistine warrior Goliath with a sling and stone (1 Samuel 17).

## Description

A life-size marble sculpture commissioned by Cardinal Scipione Borghese. Unlike Michelangelo's contemplative David, Bernini captures the biblical hero in dynamic action, mid-throw with his sling.

# The Presentation of the Virgin at the Temple

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Presentation_of_the_Virgin_at_the_Temple_(Titian))

- **Artist**: [Titian](../artists/Titian.md)
- **Location**: [Gallerie dell'Accademia](../locations/GallerieDellAccademia.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Date**: 1534-1538
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Presentation of Mary](../biblestories/PresentationInTheTemple.md) - According to tradition, Mary was presented at the Temple in Jerusalem at age three.

## Description

A huge painting (335 x 775 cm) depicting the three-year-old Virgin Mary entering the Temple of Jerusalem. Commissioned for the Scuola della Carità and still in its original location, now incorporated into the museum.

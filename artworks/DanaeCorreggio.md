# Danaë

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dana%C3%AB_(Correggio))

- **Artist**: [Correggio](../artists/Correggio.md)
- **Location**: [Galleria Borghese](../locations/GalleriaBorghese.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Date**: c. 1531
- **Source**: self-research

## Mythological Context

[Danaë](../mythology/Danae.md) - An Argive princess visited by Zeus in the form of a shower of gold, who bore the hero Perseus.

## Description

A sensuous depiction of the mythological princess Danaë receiving Zeus in the form of a shower of gold. Part of a series of paintings on the loves of Jupiter commissioned by Federico II Gonzaga.

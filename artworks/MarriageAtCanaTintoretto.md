# Marriage at Cana

- **Artist**: [Tintoretto](../artists/Tintoretto.md)
- **Location**: [Santa Maria della Salute](../locations/SantaMariaDellaSalute.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Date**: 1561
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Marriage at Cana](../biblestories/MarriageAtCana.md) - Jesus's first miracle, turning water into wine at a wedding feast in Cana of Galilee.

## Description

Displayed in the great sacristy, this painting depicts Christ's first miracle at the wedding feast. Notable for including a self-portrait of the artist among the wedding guests.

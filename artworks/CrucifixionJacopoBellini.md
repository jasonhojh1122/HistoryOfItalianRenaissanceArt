# Crucifixion

- **Artist**: [Jacopo Bellini](../artists/JacopoBellini.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Tempera on panel
- **Date**: c. 1450
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Crucifixion](../biblestories/Crucifixion.md) - The death of Jesus Christ on the cross at Golgotha.

## Description

A predella work with traditional composition from one of the founders of the Renaissance style of painting in Venice. Jacopo was the patriarch of the famous Bellini family of painters.

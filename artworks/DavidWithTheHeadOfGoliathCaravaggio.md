# David with the Head of Goliath

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_with_the_Head_of_Goliath_(Caravaggio%2C_Rome))

- **Artist**: [Caravaggio](../artists/Caravaggio.md)
- **Location**: [Galleria Borghese](../locations/GalleriaBorghese.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Date**: c. 1609–1610
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[David and Goliath](../biblestories/DavidAndGoliath.md) - Young David defeats the giant Philistine warrior Goliath with a sling and stone (1 Samuel 17).

## Description

A haunting depiction of the young David holding the severed head of Goliath. The head of Goliath is believed to be a self-portrait of Caravaggio, painted during his final years while fleeing a murder charge.

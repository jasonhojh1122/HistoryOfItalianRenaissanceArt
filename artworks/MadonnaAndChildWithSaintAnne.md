# Madonna and Child with Saint Anne (Dei Palafrenieri)

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna_and_Child_with_Saint_Anne_(Dei_Palafrenieri))

- **Artist**: [Caravaggio](../artists/Caravaggio.md)
- **Location**: [Galleria Borghese](../locations/GalleriaBorghese.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Oil on canvas
- **Date**: 1605–1606
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Madonna and Child](../biblestories/MadonnaAndChild.md) - Virgin Mary with infant Jesus.

## Description

Painted for the altar of the Papal Grooms in St. Peter's Basilica but quickly removed due to its unorthodox portrayal, including the nudity of the Christ child. Purchased by Cardinal Scipione Borghese after its rejection.

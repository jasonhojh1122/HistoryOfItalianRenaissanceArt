# Separation of Light from Darkness

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistine_Chapel_ceiling)

- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Location**: [Sistine Chapel](../locations/SistineChapel.md), Vatican City
- **Medium**: Fresco
- **Date**: 1512

## Biblical Context

[Creation](../biblestories/CreationOfAdam.md) - God separates light from darkness on the first day of creation (Genesis 1:3-5).

## Description

Completed in 1 day—the fastest execution of any panel on the ceiling. The only scene that is presented as if viewed from below, with dramatic foreshortening of God's figure. This is the first act of creation, showing God's hands separating the primordial light from darkness.

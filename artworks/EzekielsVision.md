# Ezekiel's Vision

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezekiel's_Vision_(Raphael))

- **Artist**: [Raphael](../artists/Raphael.md)
- **Location**: [Palazzo Pitti](../locations/PalazzoPitti.md), Florence
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: c. 1518
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Book of Ezekiel](../biblestories/BookOfEzekiel.md) - The prophet Ezekiel's vision of God enthroned in majesty, borne by four living creatures symbolizing the four Evangelists.

## Description

A small but powerfully composed painting depicting the prophet Ezekiel's vision of God in majesty, borne aloft by angels and the four symbols of the Evangelists. Despite its diminutive size (about 40 x 30 cm), the painting achieves monumental grandeur. It reflects the influence of Michelangelo's Sistine ceiling in its muscular figures and dynamic composition.

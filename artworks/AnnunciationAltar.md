# Annunciation Altar

- **Artist**: [Filippo della Valle](../artists/FilippoDellaValle.md)
- **Location**: [Sant'Ignazio](../locations/SantIgnazio.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Sculpture
- **Date**: 18th century
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Annunciation](../biblestories/Annunciation.md) - The announcement by the Angel Gabriel to the Virgin Mary that she would conceive and bear the Son of God.

## Description

The altarpiece in the Ludovisi Chapel dedicated to the Annunciation. The chapel houses the remains of Saint John Berchmans, a Belgian Jesuit scholastic who died while studying at the Roman College.

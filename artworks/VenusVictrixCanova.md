# Venus Victrix

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_Victrix_(Canova))

- **Artist**: [Antonio Canova](../artists/AntonioCanova.md)
- **Location**: [Galleria Borghese](../locations/GalleriaBorghese.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Marble sculpture
- **Date**: 1805–1808
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

A semi-nude, life-size reclining neo-Classical portrait sculpture of Pauline Bonaparte, Napoleon's sister and wife of Camillo Borghese. It depicts her in the guise of Venus holding the apple of Paris, reviving ancient Roman traditions of portraying mortals as deities.

# Last Supper

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Supper_(El_Greco))

- **Artist**: [El Greco](../artists/ElGreco.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Panel painting
- **Date**: 1568
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Last Supper](../biblestories/LastSupper.md) - The final meal Jesus shared with his apostles before his Crucifixion, during which he instituted the Eucharist.

## Description

An early work by El Greco from his Venetian period, before he developed the more elongated and dramatic style for which he became famous. The painting shows the influence of Venetian masters during his formative years.

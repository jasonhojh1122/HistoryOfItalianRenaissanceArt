# Madonna and Child (Frizzoni Madonna)

- **Artist**: [Giovanni Bellini](../artists/GiovanniBellini.md)
- **Location**: [Museo Correr](../locations/MuseoCorrer.md), Venice
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: c. 1470-1475
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Madonna and Child](../biblestories/MadonnaAndChild.md) - The Virgin Mary with the infant Jesus.

## Description

An innovative depiction featuring the Madonna in an unusual purple gown and pink mantle, departing from the traditional blue. Known as the Frizzoni Madonna.

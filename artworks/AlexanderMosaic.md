# Alexander Mosaic

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Mosaic)

- **Artist**: Unknown (possibly after Philoxenus of Eretria or Apelles)
- **Location**: [Pompeii Archaeological Park](../locations/PompeiiArchaeologicalPark.md), Campania (original location; now at National Archaeological Museum, Naples)
- **Medium**: Floor mosaic
- **Date**: c. 120-100 BC
- **Source**: self-research

## Historical Context

[Battle of Issus](../TERMS.md#battle-of-issus) - A battle in southern Anatolia on 5 November 333 BC between the Hellenic League led by Alexander the Great and the Achaemenid Empire led by Darius III.

## Description

Also known as the Battle of Issus Mosaic, this Roman floor mosaic was originally from the House of the Faun in Pompeii. It depicts a battle between the armies of Alexander the Great and Darius III of Persia, comprising over 50 figures with meticulous detail and captured expressions. The original is now in the National Archaeological Museum, Naples.

# Spandrel Victories and River Gods

- **Artist**: Unknown Roman sculptors (Constantinian era)
- **Location**: [Arch of Constantine](../locations/ArchOfConstantine.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Marble relief
- **Date**: c. AD 312–315
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

The spandrels (triangular areas flanking the arches) are carved with Victories bearing trophies and palm branches on the central arch, and river gods on the lateral arches. These figures follow traditional Roman triumphal iconography and provide visual continuity between the Constantinian and reused elements.

# Young Woman with Unicorn

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Woman_with_Unicorn)

- **Artist**: [Raphael](../artists/Raphael.md)
- **Location**: [Galleria Borghese](../locations/GalleriaBorghese.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Oil on panel
- **Date**: c. 1505–1506
- **Source**: self-research

## Description

A portrait of a young woman holding a small unicorn, a symbol of chastity. The composition shows the influence of Leonardo da Vinci's Mona Lisa.

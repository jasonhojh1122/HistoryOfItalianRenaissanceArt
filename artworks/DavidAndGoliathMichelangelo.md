# David and Goliath

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistine_Chapel_ceiling)

- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Location**: [Sistine Chapel](../locations/SistineChapel.md), Vatican City
- **Medium**: Fresco
- **Date**: 1509

## Biblical Context

[David and Goliath](../biblestories/DavidAndGoliath.md) - The young shepherd David defeats the giant Philistine warrior Goliath (1 Samuel 17).

## Description

One of the corner spandrels of the Sistine Chapel ceiling, depicting Old Testament scenes of salvation. Central interlocked figures create a clear narrative event—David is shown at the moment of striking down the giant Goliath. The dramatic composition emphasizes the physical struggle between the two figures.

# Sforza Chapel

- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md) (design), Tiberio Calcagni and Giacomo Della Porta (execution)
- **Location**: [Santa Maria Maggiore](../locations/SantaMariaMaggiore.md), Rome
- **Medium**: Architecture with travertine and plaster
- **Date**: c. 1564–1573
- **Source**: self-research

## Biblical Context

[Assumption of Mary](../biblestories/AssumptionOfMary.md) - The altar painting depicts the Assumption of the Virgin by [Girolamo Siciolante da Sermoneta](../artists/GirolamoSiciolanteDaSermoneta.md).

## Description

Designed by Michelangelo near the end of his life and completed posthumously by Tiberio Calcagni and Giacomo Della Porta. The chapel features an innovative elliptical plan with a three-part vault and curving side apses, representing one of Michelangelo's most forward-looking architectural experiments.

# La Rinascente

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Rinascente)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=La+Rinascente+Milan+Italy)

Milan, Italy

**Type**: Department Store
**Source**: self-research

A high-end Italian department store chain founded in Milan in 1865. The current name was coined by the poet Gabriele D'Annunzio in 1917. In 1954, the company launched the Compasso d'Oro, an Italian industrial design award. In 2016, its Milan store at Piazza del Duomo was named "Best Department Store in the World" by the IGDS.

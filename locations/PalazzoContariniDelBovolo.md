# Palazzo Contarini del Bovolo

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palazzo_Contarini_del_Bovolo)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Palazzo+Contarini+del+Bovolo+Venice+Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Palace
**Architectural style**: Gothic / Renaissance
**Source**: self-research

A small palazzo in Venice best known for its external multi-arch spiral staircase known as the Scala Contarini del Bovolo. The name "bovolo" means snail in the Venetian dialect, referring to the spiral shape. Built in the late 15th century combining Gothic and Renaissance elements.

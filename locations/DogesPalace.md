# Doge's Palace

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doge's_Palace)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Doge%27s+Palace+Venice+Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Palace / Museum
**Architectural style**: Venetian Gothic
**Source**: self-research

A palace built in Venetian Gothic style and one of the main landmarks of Venice. The palace included government offices, a jail, and the residence of the Doge of Venice. Originally built in 810, rebuilt in 1340 and extended in following centuries. It became a museum in 1923 and is one of 11 museums run by the Fondazione Musei Civici di Venezia.

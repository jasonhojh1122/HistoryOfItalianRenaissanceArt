# Aqua Virgo

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aqua_Virgo)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=Aqua%20Virgo%20aqueduct%2C%20Rome%2C%20Italy)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Roman Aqueduct
**Date**: Completed 19 BC
**Source**: self-research

One of the eleven Roman aqueducts that supplied ancient Rome, built by Marcus Agrippa during the reign of Augustus to supply the Baths of Agrippa. The aqueduct was named Virgo because legend held that a young girl led thirsty soldiers to the spring that became its source. The Trevi Fountain marks its terminus and still receives water from this ancient source (now called the Acqua Vergine).

### [Medici Chapels](../artworks/MediciChapels.md)
- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md) (Sagrestia Nuova)
- **Medium**: Architecture and marble sculpture
- **Date**: 1520-1534
- Two chapels built as an extension to the basilica. The Sagrestia Nuova (New Sacristy) was designed by Michelangelo, who served as both architect and sculptor. Contains the celebrated Medici tombs with their allegorical figures.

![img](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fb/Medicibib.jpg)

### [Tomb of Giuliano de' Medici, Duke of Nemours](../artworks/TombOfGiulianoDeMedici.md)
- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Medium**: Marble
- **Date**: 1526-1534
- Features an idealized seated portrait of Giuliano with the allegorical figures of Night and Day reclining on the sarcophagus below.

### [Tomb of Lorenzo de' Medici, Duke of Urbino](../artworks/TombOfLorenzoDeMedici.md)
- **Artist**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Medium**: Marble
- **Date**: 1524-1534
- Features a contemplative seated figure of Lorenzo (known as "Il Pensieroso") with the allegorical figures of Dawn and Dusk reclining on the sarcophagus.

### [Laurentian Library](../artworks/LaurentianLibrary.md)
- **Architect**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Date**: 1525-1571
- A historic library containing more than 11,000 manuscripts. Renowned for its Mannerist architecture featuring a dramatic vestibule with a revolutionary staircase.

### [Bronze Pulpits](../artworks/BronzePulpits.md)
- **Artist**: [Donatello](../artists/Donatello.md)
- **Medium**: Bronze
- **Date**: c. 1460-1466
- Donatello's final works, depicting scenes from the Passion and Resurrection of Christ. Completed by his workshop after his death.

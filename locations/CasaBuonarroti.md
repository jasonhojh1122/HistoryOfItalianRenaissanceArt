# Casa Buonarroti

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_Buonarroti)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Casa+Buonarroti+Florence+Italy)

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Museum

A museum dedicated to the life and work of Michelangelo, housed in a building that was once owned by the artist. The collection includes several early works by Michelangelo as well as drawings and personal items.

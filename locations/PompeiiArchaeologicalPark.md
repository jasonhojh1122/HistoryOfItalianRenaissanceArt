# Pompeii Archaeological Park

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pompeii)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=Pompeii%20Archaeological%20Park%2C%20Campania%2C%20Italy)

Campania, Italy

**Type**: Archaeological Site / UNESCO World Heritage Site
**Source**: self-research

An ancient Roman city near Naples buried under 4-6 meters of volcanic ash and pumice in the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79 AD. The preserved ruins provide an extraordinarily detailed snapshot of Roman daily life, including frescoes, mosaics, and architecture. One of Italy's most popular tourist attractions and an invaluable archaeological resource.

# Santa Maria della Salute

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria_della_Salute)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Maria+della+Salute+Venice+Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Church / Minor Basilica
**Architectural style**: Baroque
**Source**: self-research

Commonly known as La Salute, this Roman Catholic church is located at Punta della Dogana in the Dorsoduro sestiere. Built as a votive offering for Venice's deliverance from the devastating plague of 1630. Designed in Baroque style by Baldassare Longhena, completed in 1687.

**Source**: self-research

The Church of St. Ignatius of Loyola at Campus Martius is a Latin Catholic titular church dedicated to Ignatius of Loyola, founder of the Society of Jesus, located in Rome. Built in Baroque style between 1626 and 1650, it originally functioned as the chapel of the adjacent Roman College. It is one of the great 17th-century preaching churches built by Counter-Reformation orders in the Centro Storico.

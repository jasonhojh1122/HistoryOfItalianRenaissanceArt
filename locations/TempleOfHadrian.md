# Temple of Hadrian

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Hadrian)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=Temple%20of%20Hadrian%2C%20Piazza%20di%20Pietra%2C%20Rome%2C%20Italy)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Ancient Roman Temple
**Source**: self-research

An ancient Roman structure on the Campus Martius in Rome, dedicated to the deified emperor Hadrian by his adoptive son Antoninus Pius in 145 CE. Eleven of the original Corinthian columns survive, incorporated into a 17th-century building by Carlo Fontana at Piazza di Pietra, now housing Rome's Chamber of Commerce.

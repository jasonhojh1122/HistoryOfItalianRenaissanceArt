# Piazza San Marco

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_San_Marco)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Piazza+San+Marco+Venice+Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Public Square
**Source**: self-research

The principal public square of Venice, generally known just as "la Piazza." The Piazzetta extends toward the San Marco basin. Together they form the social, religious and political centre of Venice. The square is flanked by the Basilica, the Doge's Palace, the Campanile, and the Procuratie buildings.

# Museo Correr

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_Correr)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=Museo%20Correr%20Venice%20Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Museum
**Source**: self-research

The Museo Correr is a civic museum located in St. Mark's Square, Venice, extending along the southside of the square on the upper floors of the Procuratorie Nuove. It is one of the 11 civic museums run by the Fondazione Musei Civici di Venezia, housing a rich collection covering Venetian art from its Byzantine origins through the 16th century Renaissance.

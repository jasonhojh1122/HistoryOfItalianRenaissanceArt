# Bridge of Sighs

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_of_Sighs)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bridge+of+Sighs+Venice+Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Bridge
**Source**: self-research

An enclosed bridge made of white limestone with windows featuring stone bars. It passes over the Rio di Palazzo and connects the New Prison to the interrogation rooms in the Doge's Palace. Designed by Antonio Contin (whose uncle Antonio da Ponte designed the Rialto Bridge) and built in 1600. Named for the sighs of prisoners who crossed it on their way to execution.

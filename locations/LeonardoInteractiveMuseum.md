# Leonardo Interactive Museum

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Leonardo+Interactive+Museum+Via+dei+Servi+Florence+Italy)

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Museum
**Source**: self-research

A fully interactive museum in the heart of Florence's historic center dedicated to Leonardo da Vinci. Located at Via dei Servi 66/r, near the Accademia Gallery and Florence Cathedral. Features life-sized working models of Leonardo's inventions built from his Codex drawings. The collection is organized into four sections: earth, water, air, and fire.

## Exhibits

### Leonardo's Machine Models
- **After**: [Leonardo da Vinci](../artists/LeonardoDaVinci.md)
- **Medium**: Wood and metal reconstructions
- Full-scale working reproductions of machines designed by Leonardo. Visitors can interact with many machines including the tank, catapult, worm screw, hydraulic saw, and printing press.

### Flying Machine Models
- **After**: [Leonardo da Vinci](../artists/LeonardoDaVinci.md)
- **Medium**: Wood and fabric reconstructions
- Reconstructions of Leonardo's various flying machine designs representing his ambitious dream of human flight, including ornithopters and gliders based on his studies of bird flight.

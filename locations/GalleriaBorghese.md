# Galleria Borghese

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleria_Borghese)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Galleria+Borghese+Rome+Italy)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Museum
**Source**: self-research

The Galleria Borghese is an art gallery in Rome housed in the former Villa Borghese Pinciana. It houses a substantial part of the Borghese Collection of paintings, sculpture and antiquities, begun by Cardinal Scipione Borghese, the nephew of Pope Paul V.

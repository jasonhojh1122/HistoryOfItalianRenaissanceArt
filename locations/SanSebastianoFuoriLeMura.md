# San Sebastiano fuori le mura

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Sebastiano_fuori_le_mura)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=San%20Sebastiano%20fuori%20le%20mura%20Rome)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Minor Basilica
**Date**: Originally 4th century
**Source**: self-research

One of the Seven Pilgrim Churches of Rome (until the Great Jubilee of 2000). Built above the Catacombs of San Sebastiano, it honors Saint Sebastian, the Roman soldier martyred during the persecution of Christians. The church provides access to the catacombs below.

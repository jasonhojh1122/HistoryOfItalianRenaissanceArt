# Saint Mark's Basilica

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Cathedral / Basilica
**Architectural style**: Byzantine
**Source**: self-research

The Patriarchal Cathedral Basilica of Saint Mark is the cathedral church of the Patriarchate of Venice. It became the episcopal seat in 1807, replacing San Pietro di Castello. Dedicated to and holding the relics of Saint Mark the Evangelist, the patron saint of Venice. One of the finest examples of Byzantine architecture, renowned for its golden mosaics.

# Catacombs of Domitilla

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catacombs_of_Domitilla)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=Catacombs%20of%20Domitilla%20Rome)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Christian Catacomb
**Date**: 2nd-3rd centuries AD
**Source**: self-research

Among the largest catacombs in Rome, spreading over 17 km along the ancient Via Ardeatine on four levels, housing approximately 15,000 bodies. Named after the Domitilla family who commissioned them. Unique for containing an underground basilica, and features notable frescoes including early Christian iconography such as the Good Shepherd.

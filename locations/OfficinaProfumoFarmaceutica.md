# Officina Profumo-Farmaceutica di Santa Maria Novella

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Historic perfumery/shop
**Source**: self-research

A perfumery and herbalist shop in Florence, sometimes described as "the oldest pharmacy in the world." Founded by Dominican friars when they arrived in Florence in 1221, it began selling products to the public in 1612. The historic interior features Renaissance frescoes and furnishings associated with the adjacent Santa Maria Novella complex.

# Catacomb of Callixtus

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catacomb_of_Callixtus)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=Catacomb%20of%20Callixtus%20Rome)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Christian Catacomb
**Date**: 2nd-4th centuries AD
**Source**: self-research

One of the most important catacombs of Rome, containing the Crypt of the Popes with tombs of several popes from the 2nd to 4th centuries. The catacomb extends over 20 km of tunnels on four levels, reaching depths of 20 meters, and contains over 170,000 graves including sixteen pontiffs and numerous martyrs.

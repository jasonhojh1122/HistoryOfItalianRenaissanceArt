# Mercato Centrale

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercato_Centrale_(Florence))

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mercato+Centrale+Florence+Italy)

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Market
**Source**: self-research

A covered food market located in the San Lorenzo district. Built during the risanamento period when Florence was the capital of Italy in the late nineteenth century. Designed by [Giuseppe Mengoni](../artists/GiuseppeMengoni.md), the same architect who conceived the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II in Milan. The cast-iron and glass architecture from the 1870s represents an important example of 19th-century industrial design in Florence.

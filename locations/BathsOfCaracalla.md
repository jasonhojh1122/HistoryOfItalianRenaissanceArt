# Baths of Caracalla

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baths_of_Caracalla)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Baths+of+Caracalla+Rome+Italy)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Ancient Roman Ruins / Archaeological Site
**Source**: self-research

Rome's second largest Roman public baths (thermae), after the Baths of Diocletian. Built between AD 212 and 216/217 during the reigns of emperors Septimius Severus and Caracalla. The baths operated until the 530s before falling into disuse. The site yielded many famous sculptures during Renaissance excavations.

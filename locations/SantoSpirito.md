# Santo Spirito

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_di_Santo_Spirito)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santo+Spirito+Florence+Italy)

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Basilica
**Architectural style**: Renaissance

A Renaissance church in the Oltrarno quarter of Florence, designed by Filippo Brunelleschi. Construction began in 1444 but was not completed until 1487.

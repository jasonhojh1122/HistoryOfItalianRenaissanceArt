# Piazzale Michelangelo

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazzale_Michelangelo)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Piazzale+Michelangelo+Florence+Italy)

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Public square/viewpoint
**Source**: self-research

A square with a panoramic view of Florence, located in the Oltrarno district on the south bank of the Arno. Designed by architect Giuseppe Poggi in 1869 during the urban renewal when Florence was the capital of Italy.

## Artworks

### Bronze copies of Michelangelo's works
- **After**: [Michelangelo](../artists/Michelangelo.md)
- **Medium**: Bronze
- **Date**: 1873
- The piazzale features bronze copies of Michelangelo's famous works, including a copy of the David at the center and copies of the four allegorical figures (Dawn, Dusk, Day, Night) from the Medici Chapels.

# Apostolic Palace

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Palace)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Apostolic+Palace+Vatican+City)

Vatican City

**Type**: Palace
**Architectural style**: Renaissance, Baroque

The official residence of the Pope, located in Vatican City. Contains the Raphael Rooms (Stanze di Raffaello), a series of four rooms decorated with frescoes by Raphael and his workshop.

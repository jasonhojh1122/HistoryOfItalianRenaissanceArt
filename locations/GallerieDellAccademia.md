# Gallerie dell'Accademia

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallerie_dell'Accademia)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gallerie+dell%27Accademia+Venice+Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Museum
**Source**: self-research

A museum gallery of pre-19th-century art in Venice housing the world's best collection of Venetian School paintings. Located in the former Scuola della Carità on the south bank of the Grand Canal in Dorsoduro. Contains over 500 works spanning the Byzantine and Gothic periods through the Renaissance and Baroque eras.

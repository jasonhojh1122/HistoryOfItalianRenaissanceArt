# Pinacoteca Ambrosiana

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinacoteca_Ambrosiana)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pinacoteca+Ambrosiana+Milan+Italy)

Milan, Italy

**Type**: Museum

An art gallery in Milan founded by Cardinal Federico Borromeo in 1618. The collection includes works from the Renaissance and Baroque periods.

# Basilica of San Pietro in Vincoli

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pietro_in_Vincoli)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=San+Pietro+in+Vincoli+Rome+Italy)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Basilica
**Architectural style**: Renaissance

A Roman Catholic titular church and minor basilica in Rome, best known for being the home of Michelangelo's statue of Moses, part of the tomb of Pope Julius II.

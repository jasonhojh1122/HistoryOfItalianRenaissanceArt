# Tempietto

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempietto_del_Bramante)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BhFnALmMJR9Qz7yYA)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Chapel/Monument
**Architectural style**: High Renaissance

A small commemorative tomb (martyrium) built by Donato Bramante in the courtyard of San Pietro in Montorio. Completed around 1502, it marks the spot traditionally believed to be where Saint Peter was crucified. The Tempietto is considered a masterpiece of High Renaissance Italian architecture.

Marks the start of the High Renaissance in Rome.

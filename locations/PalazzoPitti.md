# Palazzo Pitti

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palazzo_Pitti)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Palazzo+Pitti+Florence+Italy)

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Palace / Museum complex
**Architectural style**: Renaissance
**Source**: self-research

The Palazzo Pitti is a vast, mainly Renaissance palace in Florence, situated on the south side of the River Arno, a short distance from the Ponte Vecchio. The core of the present palazzo dates from 1458 and was originally the town residence of Luca Pitti, an ambitious Florentine banker. Today it houses several important museums including the Palatine Gallery, the Royal Apartments, and the Gallery of Modern Art.

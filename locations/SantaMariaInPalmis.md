# Santa Maria in Palmis (Domine Quo Vadis)

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria_in_Palmis)

[GoogleMap](https://maps.google.com/?q=Santa%20Maria%20in%20Palmis%20Rome)

Rome, Italy

**Type**: Church
**Date**: Originally 9th century, rebuilt 17th century
**Source**: self-research

A small church about 800m from Porta San Sebastiano, where the Via Ardeatina branches off the Appian Way. According to the apocryphal Acts of Peter, this is where Saint Peter, fleeing persecution in Rome, met the risen Christ. Peter asked "Domine, quo vadis?" (Lord, where are you going?), and Christ answered he was going to Rome to be crucified again—prompting Peter to return and face martyrdom.

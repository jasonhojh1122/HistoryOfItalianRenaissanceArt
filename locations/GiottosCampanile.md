# Giotto's Campanile

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giotto's_Campanile)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Giotto's+Campanile+Florence+Italy)

Florence, Italy

**Type**: Bell Tower
**Architectural style**: Italian Gothic
**Source**: self-research

A free-standing campanile that is part of the complex of buildings making up [Florence Cathedral](../locations/FlorenceCathedral.md) on the Piazza del Duomo. The hexagonal panels on the lower level depict the history of mankind, inspired by Genesis, including scenes of the Creation of Adam and Eve. All present works of art on the campanile are copies; the originals were removed between 1965 and 1967 and are now in the [Museo dell'Opera del Duomo](../locations/OperaDelDuomoMuseum.md).

## Architecture

- **Begun**: 1334 by [Giotto di Bondone](../artists/GiottoDiBondone.md)
- **Continued**: [Andrea Pisano](../artists/AndreaPisano.md)
- **Completed**: 1359 by Francesco Talenti

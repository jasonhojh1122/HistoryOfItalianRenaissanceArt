# St Mark's Campanile

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mark's_Campanile)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=St+Mark%27s+Campanile+Venice+Italy)

Venice, Italy

**Type**: Bell Tower
**Source**: self-research

The bell tower of St Mark's Basilica in Venice. At 98.6 metres in height, it is the tallest structure in Venice and is colloquially termed "el paron de casa" (the master of the house). The current structure is a reconstruction completed in 1912, the previous tower having collapsed in 1902.

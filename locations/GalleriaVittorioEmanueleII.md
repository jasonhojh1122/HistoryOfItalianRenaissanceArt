# Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleria_Vittorio_Emanuele_II)

[GoogleMap](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Galleria+Vittorio+Emanuele+II+Milan+Italy)

Milan, Italy

**Type**: Historic Shopping Arcade
**Source**: self-research

Italy's oldest active shopping arcade and a major landmark of Milan. Housed within a four-story double arcade in the centre of town, the Galleria was designed in 1861 and built by architect Giuseppe Mengoni between 1865 and 1877. Named after Victor Emmanuel II, the first king of the Kingdom of Italy.
